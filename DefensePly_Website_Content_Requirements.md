# DefensePly International LLP — Website Content & Requirements

## 1. About Us

### 1.1 Company Introduction

**DEFENSEPLY INTERNATIONAL LLP** is an emerging manufacturing enterprise poised to redefine standards in the building materials industry through high-performance, eco-friendly composite solutions.

Backed by the **AP Group**, this new WPC manufacturing venture represents a strategic expansion into advanced composite building materials. The company is set to specialize in **Wood Polymer Composite (WPC)** and **Polyvinyl Chloride (PVC)** form boards, doors, and frames.

Headquartered at our **45,000 sq. ft. facility within the KINFRA Industrial Park in Kuttippuram, Kerala**, our operations are designed to harness cutting-edge manufacturing technology.

Our product line is engineered to deliver the timeless warmth and aesthetic appeal of natural wood while entirely eliminating traditional structural flaws such as water damage, termite degradation, and warping—offering sustainable, durable, and resilient alternatives for modern construction and interior architecture.

### 1.2 Company History & Strategic Vision

Founded to meet the accelerating demand for sustainable construction alternatives, DefensePly brings together decades of industrial manufacturing, architectural design, and supply chain leadership.

Operating on a **2-acre industrial site along National Highway 66**, our facility is engineered to support nationwide distribution and long-term expansion to lead India’s transition toward green architectural solutions.

### 1.3 Vision, Mission & Core Values

#### Vision Statement

> To be India’s premier manufacturer of advanced composite building materials, leading the global evolution toward zero-deforestation, sustainable eco-architecture and high-performance structural solutions.

#### Mission Statement

- **Innovation in Composite Technology:** To manufacture world-class WPC and PVC building materials utilizing state-of-the-art extrusion technology and continuous process optimization.
- **Environmental Stewardship:** To active conserve natural forests by replacing conventional timber with 100% recyclable, toxin-free composite solutions that minimize environmental impact.
- **Uncompromised Quality:** To deliver unmatched product durability, superior screw-holding capacity, and complete resistance to moisture, fire, and pests across residential, commercial, and industrial applications.
- **Customer & Partner Success:** To empower architects, contractors, interior designers, and homeowners with reliable supply chains, seamless customization, and exceptional service standards.

### 1.5 Manufacturing Infrastructure & Business Strengths

- **Facility Scale:** 45,000 sq. ft. modern manufacturing plant situated on a 2-acre plot in KINFRA Industrial Park, Moodal, Kuttippuram.
- **Advanced Machinery:** Equipped with state-of-the-art, high-precision extrusion lines. Our plant infrastructure is strategically designed with scalable capabilities to seamlessly accommodate future expansion as market demand grows.
- **Supporting Utilities:** Heavy-duty industrial utilities including high-efficiency rotary screw air compressors, industrial chillers, and a dedicated 1600 KVA power transformer.
- **Logistical Connectivity:** Situated directly on the Panvel-Kochi-Kanyakumari National Highway (NH66), close to Kuttippuram (3.3 km) and Tirur (14 km) railway stations for rapid raw material intake and nationwide product delivery.
